Frequently asked questions

How much does excavator hire cost?

Dry hire (you operate the machine) is $100 per day with a 2 day minimum. Attachments are $25 for 1 to 3, or $50 for 4 to 8. Drop-off is $50 within Sydney metro or $75 outside Sydney metro, or you can pick it up yourself at no charge. Wet hire (with one of our operators) is quoted per job, and quotes are free.

What size is the excavator?

It’s a 1.8 tonne UHI UME18 with a Kubota diesel engine, often called a mini excavator. The tracks expand from 990 mm to 1,240 mm wide, it digs to 2,150 mm deep and has a dump height of 2,310 mm. It suits most residential jobs, but it isn’t a micro machine for very tight access. If you’re unsure, send us your access width.

What’s included with dry hire?

The excavator and trailer, plus 8 attachments to choose from: 200, 400 and 600 mm digging buckets, a 1000 mm mud bucket, thumb grab, ripper, rake and quick hitch.

Do I need a licence to operate it?

No licence is needed for dry hire. Before you start, we run through the controls and safety checks with you.

Can I tow it myself?

Yes. The machine and trailer together weigh about 2,500 kg, so you’ll need a vehicle and tow bar rated to tow at least 2,500 kg, with a trailer brake controller. If you’re not sure, choose drop-off and we’ll deliver it.

What’s the minimum hire?

Dry hire is a minimum of 2 days. For shorter jobs, wet hire with one of our operators may suit you better.

How do deposits and payment work?

A $250 deposit, paid securely by card through Square, locks in your dates and comes off your total hire cost. The rest is due 24 hours before delivery. Bring photo ID on pick-up or delivery.

What if it rains or I need to cancel?

Cancellation is free with 48 hours’ notice. If wet weather stops the job, we’ll happily reschedule.

What is wet hire?

Wet hire means we supply the excavator and an experienced operator. We help with trenching and drainage, pool and spa digs, stump and hedge removal, levelling and site prep, post holes and footings, garden beds, retaining wall and slab prep, moving soil and rubble, and plumbing support. Tell us about the job for a free quote, usually within an hour.

Where are you based and where do you work?

We’re based in Londonderry in Western Sydney, and we service Sydney and the Central Coast.
